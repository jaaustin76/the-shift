THE SHIFT

PROTOTYPE v0.1 — CORE LOOP TEST

You run a sort aisle. Packages come off the trailer, ride the belt, and drop into four chutes. Each chute loads a trailer at a dock.

You have three people and four lanes. You are always one short. Tap a lane to move crew onto it — up to two each.

A chute holds 6. When it fills, packages that can't drop recirculate — they ride to the end and come back around, eating belt space. Twice around and they're pulled off to problem solve. Those don't ship.

The real skill: when a trailer departs, that dock is dead for 18 seconds. Anything arriving has nowhere to go. Drain a chute before its departure, not after.

One shift is 3 minutes. Ship as many packages as you can.
